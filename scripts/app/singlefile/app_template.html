How a reading is made

three steps, all of them in this file
  1. Name the plant. Common or scientific, one name or several. A misspelling is offered suggestions rather than quietly answered with a different species.
  2. Find its record. AUGURY looks the name up in the embedded species database and picks the region you asked for, falling back only when it tells you it has.
  3. Read it as stored. Each indicator row is quoted exactly as the database holds it, with its evidence and its source one tap away.

Indicators describe what a plant tolerates or prefers; they are a lead to look at the ground, not a measurement of it. AUGURY gives no management, herbicide or fertiliser advice.

Indicators only. AUGURY describes the soil conditions a plant indicates. It does not give management, herbicide or fertiliser advice. Every reading is quoted from the species database, with its source shown.
