# 🇦🇺 AUGURY — Australian Weed Bioindicator Deep Research (Phase 2)

**Mission:** Single-focus crawl — Australian agricultural weeds as soil health
indicators. No global scope. Only Australia.

**Why:** Our first mission returned 40+ AU species, but we need 150-200 to
train a model that is genuinely useful to Australian farmers. Every species
matters — even common ones — because training data balance depends on
quantity as well as quality.

## Sources to Target (Priority Order)

Attack these in this order:

### 1. Government Extension Publications (Rank 2-3)
These are the most authoritative and most under-used:

- **NSW DPI Primefacts** — Search "NSW DPI [weed name] primefact" for
  every weed. Many contain soil condition notes.
- **SA Landscape Board** — Hills & Fleurieu Plant Indicator Guide (already
  found, crawl deeper for regional variants: Murraylands, Eyre Peninsula,
  Kangaroo Island, Alinytjara Wilurara)
- **Victorian Resources Online** — Corangamite CMA Brown Book is
  excellent. Check Nillumbik, Mornington Peninsula, North Central, West
  Gippsland, Mallee, Wimmera versions.
- **QLD Department of Agriculture and Fisheries** — KEY GAP. Search:
  "DAF QLD weed ecology", "QLD weed indicator soil", "tropical pasture weed
  management"
- **WA DPIRD** — Key gap for ancient WA soils. Search: "WA DPIRD weed
  indicator", "wheatbelt weed bioindicator"
- **NT DITT** — Tropical weeds
- **Tasmania DPIPWE** — Cool-temperate weed indicators
- **ACT Environment** — Namadgi, Kosciuszko weeds

### 2. Industry Bodies (Rank 3-4)

- **GRDC** — Grains Research & Development Corporation. Search "GRDC weed
  soil indicator", "GRDC integrated weed management manual" — has excellent
  agronomic resources.
- **MLA** — Meat & Livestock Australia. "MLA Visual Indicators of Soil
  Condition" was already found. Search for MLA pasture-related species
  guides.
- **Cotton Australia** — Cotton-specific weed guides, especially for
  glyphosate-resistant weeds.
- **Horticulture Innovation Australia** — Horticulture weed management
  guides.
- **Sugar Research Australia** — Sugarcane weed ecology, particularly QLD.
- **Australian Hereford Society** — Pasture weed mgmt.
- **Wine Australia** — Vineyard weed ecology guides.

### 3. Peer-Reviewed Journals (Rank 1)

Search for these specific papers and similar:

- "Weed seedbank dynamics [crop system] [state]" AU context
- "Australian weed ecology [species]" 
- "Plant indicators of soil salinity Australia"
- "Pasture weed mineral content Australia"
- "Ellenberg indicator values Australia" — does anyone use them here?
- *Australian Journal of Agricultural Research*
- *Crop and Pasture Science*
- *Weed Research* (AU-focussed papers)
- *The Rangeland Journal*
- *Australian Journal of Botany*
- *Soil Research* (formerly Australian Journal of Soil Research)

### 4. Reference Books (Rank 4)

- **Parsons & Cuthbertson** "Noxious Weeds of Australia" — the bible.
  Check if it contains soil condition notes per species.
- **B. A. Auld & R. W. Medd** "Weeds: An Illustrated Botanical Guide to the
  Weeds of Australia" — 1987, comprehensive.
- **Kleinschmidt & Johnson** "Weeds of Queensland"
- **Lamp & Collet** "Weeds in Australia" — search for soil indicator data
- **Cunningham et al.** "Plants of Western New South Wales" — excellent
  source for soil relationship notes in species descriptions.
- **Moore & Williams** "Plants of the NSW Central Coast" 
- **Milthorpe** "Weeds of the Western Plains"

### 5. Practitioner / Local Knowledge (Rank 5-6)

- **Landcare Australia** — Local group publications
- **Australian Association of Bush Regenerators** — Ecological weed mgmt
- **Natural Resource Management (NRM) regional groups** — Each region has
  plans listing priority weeds
- **Permaculture Australia** — Practical weed indicator knowledge
- **Holistic Management International (AU chapter)** — Savory network in AU
- **Australian Soil Health Association** — Practitioner resources

## Key Requirements

1. **Cite every claim.** Every indicator assignment needs at least one
   re-findable source. "NSW DPI Primefact 456" is better than a URL.

2. **AU sources only for AU claims.** Do not use European/Ellenberg sources
   for Australian indicator assignments. If no AU source exists, say
   "confidence: low" with notes "No AU source found."

3. **Record EVERY species you find.** Even if the data is minimal — "common
   in wet soils" is still useful. We need quantity for training balance.

4. **QLD and WA are the highest-value gaps.** Southern AU states are partly
   covered. Northern and western species are nearly absent.

5. **Flag uncertainties honestly.** If a species is poorly studied, say so.
   "confidence: low" is valid and important — it tells us what not to train on.
