# Lamium amplexicaule — Henbit

## Indicator Profile
Winter annual consistently associated with fertile, nitrogen-rich agricultural soils. Captures early-season nutrients before crop canopy closure.

## Upgrade Rationale
Upgraded from LOW to MEDIUM confidence based on: (1) consistent association with fertile agricultural soils across multiple extension sources and continents, (2) Ellenberg N=7 for close relative *L. purpureum*.

## Caveat
Winter ephemeral — timing of observation matters. Absent in summer regardless of soil condition.
