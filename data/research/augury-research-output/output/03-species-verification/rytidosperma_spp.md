# Rytidosperma spp. — Wallaby Grasses

## Indicator Profile
Large genus of native perennial grasses. Persistence indicates low-fertility, acid soils with minimal fertilizer history. Decline under phosphorus fertilization is a well-documented response. Absence from suitable habitat signals historical nutrient enrichment.

## Recommendation
MEDIUM confidence. Include in AUGURY as a low-fertility native pasture indicator. Genus-level identification is sufficient for indicator purposes.
