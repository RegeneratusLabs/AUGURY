# Nassella trichotoma — Serrated Tussock

## Indicator Profile
Definitive low-fertility indicator in Australian grazing systems. Spreads where phosphorus and nitrogen are depleted. Control strategy universally includes fertility improvement and competitive pasture establishment.

## Upgrade Rationale
Upgraded from LOW to MEDIUM confidence based on: (1) two Australian state government agencies independently confirming low-fertility association, (2) consistent management guidance across all sources.

## Caveat
Also responds to grazing pressure — overgrazing and low fertility often co-occur, making the precise cause difficult to isolate.
