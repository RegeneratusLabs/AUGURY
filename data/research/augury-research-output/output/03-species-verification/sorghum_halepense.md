# Sorghum halepense — Johnson Grass

## Ellenberg Indicator Values (GB recalibrated)
- **L (Light):** 8 — Full light plant
- **F (Moisture):** 5 — fresh to moist (deep-rooted, drought-tolerant)
- **R (Reaction/pH):** 6-8 (wide) — weakly acid to alkaline
- **N (Nutrients):** 7 — high fertility (N=7)
- **S (Salt):** 0-1 — slight salt tolerance

*Source: Hill, M.O., Mountford, J.O., Roy, D.B. & Bunce, R.G.H. (1999). Ellenberg's Indicator Values for British Plants. ECOFACT Volume 2 Technical Annex. Institute of Terrestrial Ecology. ISBN 1-870393-48-1.*

## Key soil indicator interpretation
fresh to moist (deep-rooted, drought-tolerant). weakly acid to alkaline. high fertility (N=7). deep, well-drained, fertile.

## Nutrient relationships
N accumulator, K accumulator

## Mycorrhizal status
AM

## Regional differences
Not in Ellenberg GB data. From USDA: adaptable to wide soil range. C4 perennial. AU: restricted but serious in northern NSW/QLD. Indicates deep alluvial soil.
