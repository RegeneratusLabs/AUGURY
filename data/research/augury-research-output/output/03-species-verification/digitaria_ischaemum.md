# Digitaria ischaemum — Smooth Crabgrass

## Indicator Profile
Consistently associated with fertile, nitrogen-rich conditions across multiple US extension sources. Prefers well-drained, warm soils.

## Upgrade Rationale
Upgraded from LOW to MEDIUM confidence based on: (1) consistent association with fertile conditions across 4+ independent extension sources, (2) Ellenberg N=6 for congener *D. sanguinalis*.

## Caveat
Wide ecological amplitude — also tolerates compacted, low-fertility soils. Limited diagnostic value as a single-species indicator.
