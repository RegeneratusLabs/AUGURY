# Kummerowia striata — Japanese Clover

## Indicator Profile
As a legume, *K. striata* is an obligate nitrogen fixer. It pioneers depleted, acid soils by fixing atmospheric N. Its presence indicates functional rhizobia populations.

## Upgrade Rationale
Upgraded from LOW to MEDIUM confidence based on: (1) well-established legume N-fixation biology, (2) USDA documentation of adaptation to low-fertility acid soils.

## Caveat
Indicator relationship is complex — it responds to low N by fixing its own, not by signaling excess. Documented as a pioneer on poor soils, but also persists when fertility improves (stops nodulating).
