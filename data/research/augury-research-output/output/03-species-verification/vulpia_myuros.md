# Vulpia myuros — Rat's Tail Fescue

## Indicator Profile
Consistent low-fertility acid soil indicator in Australian grazing and cropping systems. Co-occurs with *V. bromoides* in the Victorian DPI "acid low-fertility pasture syndrome." Increasing in no-till systems.
