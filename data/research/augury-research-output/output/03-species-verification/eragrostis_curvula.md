# Eragrostis curvula — African Lovegrass

## Indicator Profile
The strongest Australian low-fertility indicator. Multiple independent sources (government, practitioner, land management) confirm: thrives on degraded, low-fertility, acid soils; recedes when fertility improves. Classified as a "fuel plant" — low mineral content, high carbohydrate.
