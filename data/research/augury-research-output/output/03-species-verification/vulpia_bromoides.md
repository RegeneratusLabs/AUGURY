# Vulpia bromoides — Silver Grass

## Indicator Profile
Definitive low-fertility acid soil indicator in Australian grazing systems. Part of the Victorian DPI-documented "acid low-fertility pasture syndrome" along with bent grass, fog grass, sweet vernal, and sorrel. Ellenberg N=2 confirms extreme infertility preference.

## Upgrade Rationale
Upgraded from LOW to MEDIUM confidence based on: (1) multiple Australian government sources independently confirming, (2) Ellenberg N=2 providing cross-continental validation, (3) consistent placement in multi-species indicator syndrome.

## Caveat
Also responds to overgrazing — difficult to separate grazing pressure from soil fertility effects.
