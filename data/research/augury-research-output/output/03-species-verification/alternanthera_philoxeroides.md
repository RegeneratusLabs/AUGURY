# Alternanthera philoxeroides — Alligator Weed

## Indicator Profile
Weed of National Significance. Proliferates in nutrient-enriched (eutrophic) waterways. Primary signal: water quality, not soil conditions. Terrestrial populations possible but less diagnostic.

## Recommendation
MEDIUM confidence as a water quality indicator. Include in AUGURY only if aquatic weed indicators are supported.
