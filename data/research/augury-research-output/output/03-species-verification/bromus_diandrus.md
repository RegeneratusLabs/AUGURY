# Bromus diandrus — Great Brome

## Indicator Profile
Strong management indicator — associated with no-till/stubble retention systems — rather than a soil indicator. Prefers sandy-loamy soils but the management signal dominates.

## Recommendation
LOW confidence as soil indicator. More useful as a farming system indicator (no-till adaptation).
