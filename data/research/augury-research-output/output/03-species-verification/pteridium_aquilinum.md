# Pteridium aquilinum — Bracken Fern

## Indicator Profile
One of the strongest fungal-dominated soil indicators in the Ducerf system. Accumulates potassium to high levels (3-4% dry weight). Ellenberg N=3 confirms low-nutrient conditions. Allelopathic — once established, suppresses competing vegetation and reinforces fungal-dominant soil biology.

## Upgrade Rationale
Upgraded from LOW to MEDIUM confidence based on: (1) consistent classification across Ducerf and Australian practitioner sources, (2) well-documented potassium accumulation in agronomic literature, (3) Ellenberg N=3 confirming low-fertility association.

## Caveat
Australian context: native *P. esculentum* may have different ecological behavior than European *P. aquilinum*.
