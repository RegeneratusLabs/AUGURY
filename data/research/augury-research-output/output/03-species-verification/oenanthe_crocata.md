# Oenanthe crocata — Hemlock Water-dropwort / Dead Tongue

## Ellenberg Indicator Values (GB recalibrated)
- **L (Light):** 7 — Light-loving plant
- **F (Moisture):** 9 — wet, water-saturated (F=9)
- **R (Reaction/pH):** 7 — neutral to weakly alkaline
- **N (Nutrients):** 7 — fertile (N=7)
- **S (Salt):** 1 — slight salt tolerance (S=1)

*Source: Hill, M.O., Mountford, J.O., Roy, D.B. & Bunce, R.G.H. (1999). Ellenberg's Indicator Values for British Plants. ECOFACT Volume 2 Technical Annex. Institute of Terrestrial Ecology. ISBN 1-870393-48-1.*

## Key soil indicator interpretation
wet, water-saturated (F=9). neutral to weakly alkaline. fertile (N=7). waterlogged, anaerobic; river banks, ditches, wet meadows, pond margins.

## Nutrient relationships
general; highly toxic species (contains oenanthotoxin)

## Mycorrhizal status
AM

## Regional differences
Ellenberg F=9, R=7, N=7, S=1. Widespread in wet habitats in W Europe; particularly abundant in SW England and Wales.
