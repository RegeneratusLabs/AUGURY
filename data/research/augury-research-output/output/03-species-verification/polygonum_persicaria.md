# Polygonum persicaria — Lady's Thumb

## Indicator Profile
Classic nitrophilous species with Ellenberg N=7. Associated with damp, nutrient-rich disturbed sites, riverbanks, and fertile arable fields.

## Upgrade Rationale
Upgraded from LOW to MEDIUM confidence based on: (1) authoritative Ellenberg N=7 value, (2) multiple independent sources confirming nitrophilous character, (3) consistent ecological niche across geographies.

## Caveat
Moisture preference is at least as strong as nutrient preference — diagnostic value limited in dry conditions.
