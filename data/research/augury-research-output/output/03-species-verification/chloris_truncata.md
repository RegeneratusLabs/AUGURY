# Chloris truncata — Windmill Grass

## Indicator Profile
Native warm-season perennial grass that increases under declining soil fertility and set stocking. GRDC classifies as potential low-fertility indicator. Drought-hardy and tolerates compacted soils.

## Recommendation
Include in AUGURY as a low-fertility indicator. Native species whose increase signals declining pasture condition.
