# Glycine tabacina — Native Soybean

## Indicator Profile
Native perennial legume indicating functional nitrogen-fixing capacity in soil microbiome. Absence from pastures where it historically occurred suggests disruption (overgrazing, P deficiency, or rhizobia loss). Prefers well-drained clay loams.

## Recommendation
Include in AUGURY as a native soil biology indicator. Absence is more informative than presence in many contexts.
