# Salvinia molesta — Salvinia

## Indicator Profile
Weed of National Significance. Floating aquatic fern indicating eutrophic, still freshwater. Successful biological control agent (*Cyrtobagous salviniae*) means presence may indicate failed or absent biocontrol, not just water quality.

## Recommendation
MEDIUM confidence as a water quality indicator. Include in AUGURY only if aquatic weed indicators are supported. Not applicable to soil systems.
