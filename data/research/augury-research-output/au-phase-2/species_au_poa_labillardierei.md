## Indicator Data

- **Waterlogging/Wetlands:** Tolerates and can indicate seasonally waterlogged soils and riparian zones — "largely survived all water treatments" in regulated river flow studies.
- **Riparian:** Common component of riparian understorey in southern and eastern Australia.
- **Soil Type:** Largely indifferent to soil type — grows on a wide range from clays to sands.
- **Drought:** Despite association with moist sites, "incredibly tough" and can survive without supplementary water.
- **Distribution:** Southern and eastern Australia (NSW, VIC, TAS, SA, southern QLD).

## Notes
- Host species for 26+ species of butterfly across Australia — significant biodiversity indicator.
- Dense, coarse tussocks to 1.2 m; provides excellent habitat for small fauna.
- Important for streambank stabilisation.
- Sometimes confused with introduced *Poa annua* and other exotic grasses.

**Confidence:** medium (AU sources confirm ecology and distribution; riparian/wetland association well-documented)
**Soil Indicators:** Waterlogging, seasonal inundation, riparian zones, streambank soils
