## Indicator Data

- **Poor/Dry Soils:** Classic indicator of poor, dry, well-drained soils — "dry ridges on poor soils" and "poor rocky soils."
- **Nitrogen-Fixing:** Presence indicates low-nitrogen soils — the plant fixes atmospheric nitrogen through Frankia symbiosis, improving soil fertility.
- **Coastal Calcareous:** "Calcareous sands by the coast" — indicator of high-pH, lime-rich coastal sands.
- **Salinity:** Tolerates soil salinity (SA Botanic Gardens) — moderate salinity indicator.
- **Soil Type:** Sand, loam, clay, limestone; well-drained essential.
- **Distribution:** NSW, VIC, TAS, SA; coastal and inland.

## Notes
- Dioecious (separate male and female trees); female trees bear large barrel-shaped cones.
- Called "sheoak" because the wood is tough and attractively grained like European oak.
- Food source for Yellow-tailed Black-Cockatoo (endangered).
- Indigenous uses: food, medicine, implements (boomerangs, shields), adhesive (canoe sealant).
- Suckering habit useful for erosion control.

**Confidence:** high (multiple AU sources confirm poor soil indicator role)
**Soil Indicators:** Poor, dry, well-drained soils; nitrogen-deficient soils; calcareous/limestone; rocky ridges
