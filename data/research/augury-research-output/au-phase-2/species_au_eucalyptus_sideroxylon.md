## Indicator Data

- **Poor/Infertile Soils:** Classic indicator of poor, shallow, infertile soils — its presence reliably indicates low-fertility sites.
- **Soil Types:** Grows on gravels, sands, ironstones, and clays — but consistently the poorest, most skeletal versions of these soil types.
- **Drainage:** Well-drained soils required; does not tolerate waterlogging.
- **Rainfall Zone:** 375–625 mm annual rainfall; moderately drought-tolerant.
- **Distribution:** Widespread in QLD, NSW, VIC; on western slopes and plains extending to coast.

## Notes
- Distinctive dark, furrowed "ironbark" persistent to small branches.
- Tree to 35 m; valuable timber and honey production species.
- High ornamental value; planted widely as street tree; the red-flowering form ('Rosea') is popular.
- The "ironbark" eucalypts as a group are often associated with poorer soils compared to other eucalypt groups.

**Confidence:** high (PlantNET and CSIRO sources explicitly confirm poor/light soil indicator)
**Soil Indicators:** Poor, shallow, infertile soils; gravels and ironstones; well-drained
