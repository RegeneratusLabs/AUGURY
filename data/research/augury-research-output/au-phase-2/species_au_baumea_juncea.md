## Indicator Data

- **Brackish/Saline Wetlands:** Grows in brackish swamps and near-coastal saltmarsh — indicator of slightly saline to brackish wetland conditions.
- **Waterlogging:** Tolerates and indicates waterlogged soils; found in freshwater to brackish meadows.
- **Coastal:** Near-coastal distribution; saltmarsh margins.
- **Soil Type:** Wetland soils; sand to clay; tolerates seasonal inundation.
- **Distribution:** Southern Australia (WA, SA, VIC, NSW, TAS); coastal and near-coastal.

## Notes
- Now classified as *Machaerina juncea* in some treatments.
- Excellent for wetland bank stabilisation.
- Distinguished from other Baumea/Machaerina species by its leafless, cylindrical stems ("bare twig").

**Confidence:** high (VRO Victoria source explicitly classifies it by salinity class and waterlogging)
**Soil Indicators:** Brackish wetlands, coastal saltmarsh, waterlogging, seasonal inundation
