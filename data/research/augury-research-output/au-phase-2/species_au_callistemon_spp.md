## Indicator Data

- **Watercourses/Riparian:** Primary indicator of watercourses — "widespread along watercourses, dried and rocky riverbeds and gullies."
- **Waterlogging/Inundation:** Tolerates severe seasonal waterlogging and inundation — presence reliably indicates areas subject to seasonal flooding.
- **Poor/Acidic Soils:** Also tolerant of poor and acidic soils, suggesting presence may also indicate low-fertility riparian zones.
- **Poor Drainage:** Tolerates and indicates poorly drained sites.
- **Distribution:** Eastern and southern Australia — QLD, NSW, VIC, SA, TAS.

## Key Species
- *Callistemon sieberi* (syn. *Melaleuca sieberi*) — River Bottlebrush
- *Callistemon paludosus* — River Bottlebrush (sometimes treated as synonym or separate)
- *Callistemon viminalis* — Weeping Bottlebrush (creek lines)
- *Callistemon salignus* — Willow Bottlebrush (moist sites)

## Notes
- Many Callistemon spp. now taxonomically moved to Melaleuca, but horticultural usage retains Callistemon.
- Important riparian stabilisation species.
- Tolerates fire and resprouts — well-adapted to fire-prone riparian zones.

**Confidence:** medium (AU sources confirm watercourse/waterlogging association; limited formal "indicator" documentation)
**Soil Indicators:** Watercourses, seasonal waterlogging/inundation, poor drainage, acidic soils
