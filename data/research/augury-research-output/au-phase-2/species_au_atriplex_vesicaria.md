## Indicator Data

- **Salinity:** Strong indicator of saline soils across inland Australia; good salt tolerance.
- **Pasture Condition:** Considered a reliable indicator of pasture condition in the rangelands — presence of healthy bladder saltbush communities indicates well-managed grazing systems.
- **Drought/Aridity:** Indicator of arid and semi-arid zones; sheds leaves under extreme drought to conserve moisture.
- **Soil Type:** Grows on a range of soil types including loams, clays, and stony soils in the 200–400 mm rainfall zone.
- **Distribution:** Native to inland Australia — widespread across NSW, SA, VIC, QLD, WA, NT.

## Notes
- Perennial shrub to ~1 m; highly palatable and important forage species in rangeland grazing.
- Subspecies macrocystidia occurs near Balranald (NSW).
- Key species for rangeland condition assessment methodology.
