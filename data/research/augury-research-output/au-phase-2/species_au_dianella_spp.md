## Indicator Data

- **Native Understorey Intactness:** Presence of Dianella in woodlands and forests indicates intact native understorey — can be the dominant understorey species in undisturbed habitats.
- **Well-Drained Soils:** Prefers well-drained soils; absence in otherwise suitable habitat may indicate waterlogging or soil degradation.
- **Woodland/Forest:** Indicator of healthy woodland and forest ecosystems across Australia.
- **Soil Type:** Well-drained soils preferred; tolerates a range from sandy to loamy.
- **Distribution:** Australia-wide; about 20 species endemic to Australia.

## Key Species
- *Dianella revoluta* — Black-anther Flax Lily
- *Dianella caerulea* — Blue Flax Lily
- *Dianella tasmanica* — Tasman Flax Lily
- *Dianella longifolia* — Pale Flax Lily

## Notes
- Indigenous uses: leaves for weaving/string; berries edible (some species).
- The blue-purple berries and flowers are distinctive; seeds produce an indigo stain.
- Important component of bushland understorey biodiversity.

**Confidence:** low (AU sources confirm ecology but limited formal indicator documentation for soil properties)
**Soil Indicators:** Well-drained soils, intact native woodland/forest understorey
