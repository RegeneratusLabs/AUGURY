## Indicator Data

- **Salinity:** Indicator of saline soils — cultivated for saline land restoration programs.
- **Aridity:** Drought-tolerant; native to inland Australia.
- **Soil Type:** Tolerates a wide range of soil conditions including saline, alkaline, and well-drained soils.
- **Growth Form:** Perennial shrub to ~1.5 m; spiny branch tips; silvery-grey foliage.
- **Distribution:** Widespread across inland Australia (all mainland states).

## Notes
- Also known as Creeping Australian Saltbush; sometimes cultivated as an ornamental hedge for difficult sites.
- Commercial cultivars available ('SAB01' PBR, etc.) for landscaping and land restoration.
- Member of the saltbush community; important understorey species in arid and semi-arid woodlands.

**Confidence:** medium (AU sources confirm salinity association, but less documented as formal indicator than Atriplex spp.)
**Soil Indicators:** Salinity, drought-prone soils, alkaline soils
