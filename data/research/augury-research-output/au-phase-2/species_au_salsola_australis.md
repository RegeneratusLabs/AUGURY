## Indicator Data

- **Salinity:** Salsola species are classic salinity indicators across inland Australia. S. australis/tragus complex is found on saline swamps, lake beds, and disturbed saline sites.
- **Disturbance:** Associated with overgrazed and disturbed rangeland, particularly on heavier soils.
- **Arid Zone:** Widespread in semi-arid and arid zones of all mainland states.
- **Growth:** Annual or short-lived perennial; tumbleweed dispersal habit.

## Caveats
- Taxonomic complexity: the Australian Salsola complex includes native S. australis, but introduced Eurasian species (e.g., S. tragus, S. kali) may also be present. Differentiation in the field can be difficult.
- Salsola sabrinae Mosyakin was recently proposed as a distinct Australian native species.

**Confidence:** medium (AU sources exist but taxonomic complexity and potential introduced species confounding)
**Soil Indicators:** Salinity, disturbed soils, heavy clays
