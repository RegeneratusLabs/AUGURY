## Indicator Data

- **Salinity:** Primary indicator of saline soils; tolerant of high soil salinity and moderately saline groundwater.
- **Aridity:** Indicator of low-rainfall zones (250–500 mm annual rainfall), well-drained soils.
- **Soil Type:** Naturally occurs on heavier textured soils (clays, clay loams); also grows on a range of soil types.
- **Waterlogging:** Does NOT tolerate waterlogging — presence indicates well-drained saline sites specifically.
- **Distribution:** Widespread across inland Australia, all mainland states.

## Notes
- Deep-rooted perennial shrub to 3 m; used in commercial saltland pasture systems.
- Important forage species in saline agricultural landscapes — planted for grazing on otherwise unproductive land.
- CSIRO has released improved cultivars for saline land rehabilitation.
