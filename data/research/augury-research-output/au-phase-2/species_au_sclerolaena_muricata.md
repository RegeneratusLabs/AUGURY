## Indicator Data

- **Overgrazing/Disturbance:** Classic indicator of overgrazed rangeland — increases under heavy grazing pressure as more palatable species decline.
- **Soil Type:** Heavy clays and heavier textured soils; also on disturbed sites and areas subject to inundation.
- **Arid/Semi-arid Zone:** Perennial shrub native to eastern and central Australia.
- **Growth Form:** Low shrub with tangled, brittle branches that become rigid with age; tumbleweed dispersal of fruiting bodies.

## Notes
- Perennial shrub native to eastern and central Australia.
- The name "roly poly" refers to the detached fruiting bodies that tumble in the wind, dispersing seed.
- Increasing abundance often indicates declining rangeland condition and overgrazing.

**Confidence:** high (multiple AU sources confirm overgrazing/disturbance indicator role)
**Soil Indicators:** Overgrazed pastures, heavy clay soils, disturbed sites, inundation areas
