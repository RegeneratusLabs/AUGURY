## Indicator Data

- **Salinity:** Moderate salinity indicator — usually found on slightly saline soils.
- **Arid Zone:** Strong indicator of Australian arid lands; short-lived perennial adapted to low and variable rainfall.
- **Waterlogging:** Poor waterlogging tolerance — presence indicates well-drained sites even in low-lying areas.
- **Soil Type:** Wide variety of soil types; often on slightly saline, well-drained soils.
- **Distribution:** Endemic to Australia; widely distributed across arid and semi-arid zones.

## Notes
- Small shrub, typically <1 m; member of Chenopodiaceae (now Amaranthaceae).
- Part of the saltbush-bluebush shrubland community that dominates large areas of the Australian rangelands.
- Valuable forage species but short-lived; responds to rainfall events.

**Confidence:** medium (AU sources confirm moderate salinity association, limited dedicated indicator research)
**Soil Indicators:** Slight salinity, well-drained, arid zone soils
