## Indicator Data

- **Impeded Drainage:** Primary indicator of areas with impeded drainage — L. longitudinale forms dense stands in such conditions across southern Australia.
- **Wetland Health:** Presence of Lepidosperma indicates healthy, functioning wetland systems — provides microhabitats, stabilises soil, enhances water quality.
- **Soil Type:** Sand/clay soils in wetland areas; fresh water quality required.
- **Distribution:** Southern Australia (WA, SA, VIC, NSW, TAS); coastal and near-coastal.

## Key Species
- *Lepidosperma gladiatum* — Coast Sword-sedge
- *Lepidosperma longitudinale* — Pithy Sword Sedge
- *Lepidosperma tetraquetrum* — Angle Sword Sedge

## Notes
- Important sand stabilisation role on coastal dunes and verges.
- Dense stands provide critical habitat and food sources for fauna.
- Sharp-edged leaves characteristic of the genus.

**Confidence:** medium (AU sources confirm wetland/healthy system indicator role)
**Soil Indicators:** Impeded drainage, freshwater wetlands, sand/clay soils, healthy wetland function
