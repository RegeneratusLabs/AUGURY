## Indicator Data

- **Soil Type:** Strongest indicator of heavy grey cracking clay soils (vertisols) in northern Australia. These soils store subsoil moisture critical for summer-growing Mitchell grass.
- **Pasture Condition:** Dominance of Mitchell grass indicates good pasture condition; decline and replacement by annual grasses and weeds indicates overgrazing.
- **Overgrazing:** Only palatable when very young — heavy grazing reduces/eliminates Mitchell grass. However, it responds well after grazing rest with summer rainfall.
- **Rainfall Zone:** 250–550 mm annual rainfall; summer-dominant rainfall pattern.
- **Distribution:** Extensive grasslands across QLD, northern NSW, NT, and northern WA.

## Key Species
- *Astrebla lappacea* — Curly Mitchell grass (most widespread)
- *Astrebla pectinata* — Barley Mitchell grass
- *Astrebla squarrosa* — Bull Mitchell grass
- *Astrebla elymoides* — Hoop Mitchell grass

**Confidence:** high (extensive AU government and research documentation)
**Soil Indicators:** Heavy grey cracking clays (vertisols), summer moisture storage, good pasture condition
